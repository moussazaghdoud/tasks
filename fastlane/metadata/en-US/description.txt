Hence is a task manager built around one idea: write first, organize later, act fast.

Tap the microphone and say what needs doing — "call Thierry tomorrow about the partner terms, it's urgent" — and Hence turns it into a clear task: a short title, the date and time, the priority, the project, the person you delegated it to. A rambling memo comes back short and sharp, with a two-sentence summary of what was worth keeping. You confirm before anything is created, and you can change any detail first.

Prefer to type? The same understanding works when you write. "Board deck friday high priority" needs no forms, no menus, no date pickers.

THE HOME SCREEN ANSWERS ONE QUESTION
What now? A greeting, the microphone, and your next three tasks. Everything else waits behind More — the rest of the day, the week ahead, what you finished. No dashboard, no widgets, no badges.

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Today, Upcoming, Inbox, Important and Later — time first, folders second
• Lightweight projects, notes, steps, links and recurring tasks
• Reminders that arrive even when the app is closed
• Focus mode: one task, its steps and a timer
• Triage: sort a full inbox with one tap per task
• Swipe to complete, drag to reorder, undo anything

QUIET BY DESIGN
No accounts. No advertising. No trackers or analytics. Your tasks stay on your device, and you can export them whenever you like.
