Hence keeps the thoughts that would otherwise slip away.

Tap the orb and say what's on your mind. Hence writes it down as one clear line, ready the moment you look back. Say "remind me tomorrow at nine thirty" and the reminder is set as you speak. Dictating a list? Say "new line". Prefer not to talk? Type it, or take a photo — a picture can be the whole thought.

PHOTOS YOU CAN SEARCH
Photograph a whiteboard, a business card or a label: Hence reads the words in it, right on your iPhone. Search for a name or a number and the photo comes back.

TURN A THOUGHT INTO WHAT IT NEEDS TO BE
Tap any line to mark it done or important, set a reminder for any date and time, make it repeat, turn it into an email, add it to your calendar, or share it. Swipe left to delete — and undo it if you did not mean to.

BUSINESS AND PRIVATE, APART
Work thoughts and personal ones live side by side without mixing. Move a thought from one to the other with a tap.

YOUR WEEK AT A GLANCE
Connect Outlook or Google Calendar and an Agenda appears: the week ahead, a colour per day. Tap a meeting to cancel it if it is yours, decline it if it is not, or email the organiser.

SPEAK THE WAY YOU SPEAK
Hence understands English, French and Simplified Chinese, and can listen in all of them at once and keep whichever reads right. The interface comes in the same three languages.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account to create. No advertising, no tracking, no analytics. Your thoughts are kept on your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud so a new phone finds them — we never see them. If you choose, the text of a voice note — never the audio — can be sent to an AI model to tidy it into one clean line; Hence asks before the first one, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Dark and light, designed for one hand.
