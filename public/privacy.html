Privacy Policy

Hence — last updated 3 October 2026

Hence keeps the thoughts you speak or type as a simple list. It has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. This page explains exactly what happens to what you write or say.

What stays on your device

Your notes, the people and projects they mention, and your settings are stored on the device you use — in the app's own storage on iOS, or in your browser's storage on the web. They are never sent to us, and we cannot read them. On iOS a copy can also be kept in your own iCloud (see below). Deleting the app deletes what is on the device. In Settings you can also save a backup file, which you keep wherever you choose, and restore from it.

A copy in your iCloud (iOS)

If iCloud Drive is on for your iPhone, the app keeps a copy of your notes and their photos in your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app or change iPhone.

  • The copy is stored in the app's private folder of your iCloud, under your Apple ID, by Apple. It counts against your iCloud storage, not ours.
  • It never passes through the Hence server, and we have no access to it.
  • It is updated a few seconds after each change. It is only read when the app starts with no notes, to bring them back.
  • To stop it, turn off iCloud Drive for Hence in the iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud. You can delete the copy there too, under Manage Storage.
  • If iCloud is off, nothing is copied and everything stays on the device.

Turning speech into text

When you use the microphone, your speech is transcribed by the operating system or browser you are using, not by us:

  • In the iOS app: Apple's speech recognition. Apple may process the audio on its servers. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • In a browser: that browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari).

We do not record, store or transmit audio ourselves.

So that names are spelled correctly, the iOS app gives Apple's speech recognition a short list of words it is likely to hear with each recording: the names of people and projects in your list, and names that appear in your open notes. It is sent to Apple with the audio and used only to transcribe it.

Photos (optional)

If you tap the camera button, the photo you take is kept with that note, on this device only:

  • The photo is written into the app's own private storage. It is not added to your camera roll, and no other app can read it.
  • The words in the photo are read on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in text recognition, so you can search for them. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.
  • It is never sent to us. It is not sent to the Hence server, and it is not sent to Claude — only the sentence you speak is, and only if you allowed that. If iCloud is on, it is copied to your own iCloud with your notes (see above).
  • Deleting the note deletes the photo; the app removes orphaned photos the next time it starts.
  • The app asks for camera permission the first time, and never reads your photo library.

Turning a memo into a task

To turn what you said into a clear task, the app sends the text of your memo to the Hence server, which forwards it to one AI service and returns the result: either Anthropic's API (the Claude model) or Google's Gemini API. The server uses one of them for everyone at a time, and the app names the one in use before it asks you, and in Settings → Voice notes. If the service changes, the app asks you again before sending anything to the new one. To make the result fit your work, the request also includes the names of your projects, the names of people you have added, and the titles of your open tasks.

  • The iOS app asks before sending anything to Claude, the first time you record, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Voice notes. If you decline, your notes are read on your device and nothing is sent.
  • The Hence server does not store your memo or the result. It passes them through and keeps only anonymous error logs.
  • Your IP address is used, in memory only, to limit how many requests one connection can make. The Hence server does not store or log it; the hosting provider may keep standard access logs.
  • Anthropic or Google processes the request to produce the answer. Hence uses their paid API services, under which inputs are not used to train their models; they may be retained for a limited time for abuse monitoring under each company's terms.
  • If no analysis service is configured, or you are offline, the memo is analysed on your device and nothing leaves it.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device and delivered by it. They are not sent through any server and are not shared between devices.

Your calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect a calendar in Settings — a Microsoft 365 or Outlook account, a Google account, or both — the app can show your upcoming meetings and add a note to your calendar as an event. When you tap a meeting and confirm, it can also cancel a meeting you organise (your calendar then tells the attendees) or decline one you were invited to (the organiser is told). It only does this when you ask. This connection is between your device and Microsoft or Google directly; it does not pass through the Hence server.

  • You sign in on Microsoft's or Google's own page. The app never sees your password.
  • The app asks only for access to your calendar and your basic profile, so it can show which account is connected. It cannot read your email or files.
  • The key that keeps you connected is stored in your device's Keychain, encrypted by iOS and readable by no other app.
  • Your upcoming meetings — their titles, times and organisers — are kept on the device so they appear instantly and offline. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • "Email the organiser" opens your own Mail app with their address filled in; nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Disconnecting in Settings removes the key and the stored meetings from the device. You can also revoke access from your Microsoft or Google account at any time.

What we never do

  • No account, so nothing is tied to your identity.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs, no behavioural profiling, no selling of data.

Children

Hence is not directed at children and collects no information about them.
