Privacy Policy

Hence — last updated 17 September 2026

Hence is a task manager. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. This page explains exactly what happens to what you write or say.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, projects, notes, the people you add and your settings are stored on the device you use — in the app's own storage on iOS, or in your browser's storage on the web. They are not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot read them. Deleting the app (or “Start empty” in Settings) deletes them. You can export a copy at any time from Settings → Export data.

Turning speech into text

When you use the microphone, your speech is transcribed by the operating system or browser you are using, not by us:

  • In the iOS app: Apple's speech recognition. Apple may process the audio on its servers. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • In a browser: that browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari).

We do not record, store or transmit audio ourselves.

Turning a memo into a task

To turn what you said into a clear task, the app sends the text of your memo to the Hence server, which forwards it to Anthropic's API (the Claude model) and returns the result. To make the result fit your work, the request also includes the names of your projects, the names of people you have added, and the titles of your open tasks.

  • The Hence server does not store your memo or the result. It passes them through and keeps only anonymous error logs.
  • Anthropic processes the request to produce the answer. Inputs sent through their API are not used to train their models; they may be retained briefly for abuse monitoring under Anthropic's terms.
  • If no analysis service is configured, or you are offline, the memo is analysed on your device and nothing leaves it.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your device and delivered by it. They are not sent through any server and are not shared between devices.

What we never do

  • No account, so nothing is tied to your identity.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs, no behavioural profiling, no selling of data.

Children

Hence is not directed at children and collects no information about them.
