Support

Hence turns what you say into a clear task. Tap the microphone, speak, and confirm the task it proposes. If you would rather write, use or type under the microphone.

The microphone hears nothing

iOS asks for two permissions the first time: the microphone and speech recognition. If you declined either, open Settings → Hence on your iPhone and turn them on. Speech recognition also needs a network connection.

Reminders don't arrive

Reminders are delivered by iOS, so notifications must be allowed: Settings → Hence → Notifications. They are scheduled on the device and do not follow you to another one.

My tasks are not on my other device

Tasks are stored on the device you created them on. To move them, use Settings → Export data and import the file on the other device.

Something else

Write to REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL and describe what happened and what you expected. If it is about a specific task, the app's Export data file helps.
